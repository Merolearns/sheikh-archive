## Decisions

### Q1. Login page (brief item 15)

**D1. Guest is the primary button; Google sign-in is secondary. (4–2)** Claude Code, Codex, DeepSeek, and Kimi put guest first; Gemini and Qwen put sign-in first. The decision-taker agrees with the majority. This reverses the brief's listed order ("sign in with Gmail, or enter as a guest") explicitly, and it follows playbook §2.14 ("never force account creation before the action") and the v4 opening's single-Enter pattern. The opening stays a way in, never a wall.

**D2. Button labels (proposed wording; the item-8 native-Arabic copy audit has final say).**
- Guest, EN: **"Continue as guest"** (CC, DS, QW, GM; CX "Enter as guest", KM "Enter the Library" recorded as minorities).
- Guest, AR: **"الدخول كضيف"** (CX, QW). Qwen, who owns the Arabic judgment in this round, rules it the natural native form for an entry button, better than "المتابعة كزائر". Claude Code's "ادخل زائرًا" and DeepSeek's "المتابعة كزائر" are recorded as the alternatives for the audit to confirm.
- Sign-in, EN: council recommends **"Sign in with Google"** (CC, DS, GM, KM) over "Sign in with Gmail" (QW, CX), 4–2, on Google's branding rules (the provider is Google; Gmail is the mail product). **Owner decision required:** the owner said "Gmail" twice on the call. The council's recommendation is Google; the owner confirms the label before item 15 is built (see blockers).
- Sign-in, AR: "تسجيل الدخول باستخدام Google" (CC, DS, KM) as the proposed form; Qwen's "تسجيل الدخول عبر Gmail" follows the Gmail label if the owner confirms Gmail.

**D3. Layout (DESIGN).** Single centered column over the painted sunset, in this order: Kufi emblem, verse, Sheikh's name, then the stacked pills, then one quiet helper line. Both pills 52px tall, full pill radius, full width on mobile, max width 360px, 12px gap between them. Verse in Amiri Quran, line-height **2.2** (CC, CX, KM; DS and QW's 2.0 recorded as the floor — 2.2 matches v4's `.sp-verse`, verified by Codex). The buttons are clickable from the first frame; the fade is visual only and never blocks interaction (CC). One quiet helper line under the pills, muted, 14–15px: "Your progress is saved on this device. Sign in to keep it with your account." / "يُحفظ تقدّمك على هذا الجهاز. سجّل الدخول لحفظه في حسابك." (CC's wording adopted; DS/QW equivalents recorded in their files).

**D4. Offline sign-in (DESIGN).** The Google button stays in place but disabled, label "Needs an internet connection" / "يتطلب اتصالًا بالإنترنت". The layout never shifts. Guest entry works fully offline (CC, QW).

**D5. Return visits (DESIGN).** A valid signed-in session skips the opening and goes straight to Home (CC). The welcome line ("Welcome back" / "أهلاً بعودتك") appears on Home, not on the opening (DS). Kimi's opening welcome line is not adopted: the opening stays sacred and minimal; progress and greeting belong on Home. The opening comes back on sign-out or from the emblem.

**D6. Progress after entry (DESIGN).** Home row 1 is "Continue watching" / "تابع المشاهدة": up to 6 cards, newest first, each with a 3px gold progress line along the bottom, a resume timestamp, and "min left" (CC, QW, DS, CX). Home row 2 is "Series you started" / "سلاسل بدأتها": a card per playlist with a fraction ("4 of 20 lessons" / "٤ من ٢٠ درسًا") and a progress line (CC, DS). Where to Start steps show per-step progress once started (QW). Either row with no items is hidden, never shown empty (all). **No "My Progress" tab inside Saved** (Gemini's proposal rejected: Saved stays video-only by planning decision; progress lives on Home, series cards, and Where to Start).
- Half-watched means started and not complete: position above zero and below the watched threshold. **Watched at 90%** (CC, decision-taker) with manual "Mark as watched" overriding; DeepSeek's 95% recorded as the minority. The item-2 gate verifies the exact threshold on real playback.
- Account chip in the fixed chrome, next to the EN|AR toggle, 36–44px: first name or a single initial letter in a circle — **no remote avatar, no Google photo, no network call** (CC, DS, QW, KM read together). Guests see "Guest" / "ضيف". Tapping the chip opens a small menu (greeting, sign-in / sign-out, merge re-offer) and nothing else.

**D7. Merge on first sign-in (DESIGN).** Offered once, when the visitor signs in and local guest progress exists on the device. A modal/dialog in the Illuminated Page language (GM, KM, QW; DS's inline variant recorded as the minority). Default is Merge. **Declining keeps local progress on the device only, untouched, forever** — Gemini's "cloud data overwrites local on decline" is REJECTED: it contradicts brief item 15 ("signing in must never wipe or overwrite local progress"). Merge rule: union of started courses, furthest position per video, completed wins (DS, QW, CC). If the account already has newer progress for the same video, the newer position is kept and the dialog says so in one line (QW). Signing out returns to the opening and leaves both account and local progress untouched (QW). **No 24-hour undo control in Settings** (Kimi's proposal flagged: v4 has no Settings surface; the merge is re-offered from the account menu on next sign-in instead).

**D8. Sign-in failure and cancellation (DESIGN).** Failure shows an inline quiet notice under the Google pill ("Could not sign in. Try again." / "تعذّر تسجيل الدخول. حاول مرة أخرى."); the guest pill stays fully usable; no retry loop, no blocking spinner (CC, DS, QW, KM over GM's bottom-toast). A cancelled chooser is **silent** (KM): cancellation is neutral, not an error; DeepSeek's "sign-in was cancelled" string is not adopted.

### Q2. Whole-project design review

**D9. v4 stays as the base. (6–0)** No screen is redesigned for its own sake. What changes in v5 is confined to the new surfaces, the audit leftovers, and the login page (QW).

**D10. Type and spacing (DESIGN, Claude Code's scale adopted).** Arabic body Noto Sans Arabic 17px/1.8; English body 16px/1.6; headings Amiri Bold, H1 24px, H2 20px; line length at most 60 characters Arabic, 68 English; 8px grid; card radius 12px; focus ring 2px with 2px offset, contrast at least 3:1 in every theme. Transcript and About body max-width 680px (DS).

**D11. Numerals: Arabic-Indic digits in Arabic UI, Western digits in English UI. (5–1)** CC, GM, DS, QW plus the v4 rule; durations keep the LTR mm:ss shape with Arabic-Indic digits inside it. Kimi's "Latin digits (0-9) across both EN and AR UI" is REJECTED: it contradicts v4 DECISIONS.md action 10 and the v4-improvements report. Kimi's plural forms themselves (درسان / 3 دروس / كتابان / 5 كتب) are consistent with the v4 `Intl.PluralRules` helper and stand.

**D12. Opening verse motion: one fade+rise, 400ms. (Decision-taker's call; split recorded.)** CC decides 400ms; DS and QW give 700ms. The split is recorded plainly: 400ms adopted, 700ms is the minority. Gold line draw 350ms once per session (DS). No other ambient motion on the opening; under Calm or prefers-reduced-motion the opening collapses to one static, fully composed frame.

**D13. Transcript panel (DESIGN).** Below the player, AR|EN tabs (44px), the last tab chosen remembered per lesson, body 16px/1.7, max-width 680px. The active line is marked by a 2px gold line on its leading edge, never a gold fill (CC). Tap a line to seek the local player; in YouTube mode the panel stays available with full text readable, line sync verified at build time, manual scroll as the fallback with a quiet "reading only" notice (QW, KM). **No side-by-side panes** (QW corrected its own proposal: the v4 panel stands). **Gemini's 1.2 line-height is REJECTED**: it clips tashkeel; the research requires 1.7–1.8 and transcripts carry quoted Qur'an.

**D14. Dual playback player (DESIGN).** One 16:9 player on the lesson page. A segmented source switcher lists only the sources this lesson actually has, labels **YouTube / Saved copy / Local file** (CC). The primary action on click is YouTube in-page; its label is **"Watch on YouTube" / "مشاهدة على يوتيوب"** (the planning verdict's agreed wording — it stands; Kimi's "Play lesson" / "شغّل الدرس" is not adopted as the primary label, and the 390px/desktop labels must not diverge). Save and Mark as watched sit beside the switcher. Switching source keeps the current time; activating YouTube pauses local playback, saves position, stops recitation. **Gemini's icon-only "Source Toggle" is REJECTED**: text labels are mandatory (planning decision 4) and "Server" is technical jargon the round-3 simplicity rule forbids. Kimi's "Other sources" disclosure is not adopted (3–1 for the visible switcher: CC, CX, QW): the fallback the owner wants visible must not be hidden behind a disclosure. Unavailable sources are omitted from the switcher, never shown disabled (QW, CC).

**D15. Books (DESIGN).** Hidden until at least one approved book exists WITH its readable file; no 6th nav item; reached from a secondary link in the Library header and from About, labelled "الكتب" (all). Series-card grid (cover or khatam emblem, Arabic title, English title if supplied, language badge, page count; owner order, then Arabic title; language filter only). The Sheikh's PhD book lists as one title with four ordered parts once approved (CX). No cover uses the khatam emblem (Kimi's Reem Kufi initial tile recorded as the builder's alternative). Native PDF reader with "Open file" fallback; preview flavour metadata only ("Available in the installed app" / "متاح في التطبيق المثبت"). Saved stays video-only; books do not go on Where to Start; search indexes titles and metadata only, results grouped (lessons first, books second).

**D16. About (DESIGN).** The About hub stays visible exactly as in v4 (three tabs: archive, Mission, The Sheikh). **Codex's "keep the route out of navigation until approved content exists" and Kimi's "hide the About link and route completely" are both REJECTED**: either would hide the About and Mission tabs, which do not depend on bio facts, and would regress v4. The decided rule is the existing v4 one: publish only owner-approved facts inside the hub; omit unapproved sections; the Sheikh tab's bio block is omitted when no verified facts exist. "Dr." omitted until verified with the owner and a publishable source. The proposed static line stays a proposal needing owner approval and a source. Books list linked once populated.

**D17. 44px targets and the 390px breakpoint (DESIGN, Codex mechanical).** Codex verified firsthand in `v4/src`: `.btn` passes at 44px, but `.nav a` is 38px, `.seg button` 32px, `.btn.small` 36px, `.lacts .btn` 38px — all below 44px; and there is no 390px media query (smallest breakpoint is 600px), so the v4 390px claim rests on screenshots, not a CSS rule. V5 fixes every sub-44px control to at least 44px actual hit area, adds a 390px breakpoint, and at 480px and below uses a 44px menu trigger with 44px rows. At 390px: no horizontal page scroll, pills stack full width, Books grid is one column, transcript panel full width below the player, merge dialog full width with 16px margins and stacked buttons.

**D18. Themes (DESIGN).** Qwen's corrected token set stands: Parchment (default light), Emerald (default dark), Midnight, Sand, Sunset/Maghrib (dark only, never the default), Pure dark (accessibility theme). Gold stays a 1–2px line, rule, ring, or progress bar in every theme. Contrast gates: text 4.5:1, gold line and focus ring 3:1, verse 7:1 as the target (DS) with 4.5:1 as the floor. A theme that changes the hero but not the player is a failed theme. Theme switch cross-fade 320ms; the picker stays the visual swatch picker with live preview from v4, with the 390px clipping fixed (not moved into a settings gear).

**D19. Motion inside the library (DESIGN).** The round-3 and v4 motion specs stand (QW's detailed list is the reference): home entrances fade+rise 8px with 70ms stagger capped at 8; rail medallion dividers draw once per session; card hover lift 3px plus gold rule 180ms; press scale 0.97 in 90ms; View-Transition page changes 380ms with frozen chrome and a CSS fallback; language toggle thumb slides 250ms with chrome never moving; save star pop 240ms plus one quiet gold sparkle; transcript golden thread FLIP 240ms. Ambient stays slow (gradient drift 16–20s, emblem rotation 40–60s or 4s pulse, never both, one ambient motion per screen). Verse text is never animated inside the library. Calm and prefers-reduced-motion collapse every entrance, stagger, draw-on, and ambient motion to its final static frame; the buffering ring becomes a static icon with a label; skeletons go static.

**D20. Pattern budget (DESIGN).** One khatam geometry at five scales, nothing else. Hero wash 5–7% gold, medallion dividers 28–32px between rails, double-rule frames on featured cards only, player frame and transcript header medallion, dialog medallions drawing once, footer medallion, stats-band girih at 5%. No lattice behind grids, transcript body, or verse. No pattern on errors beyond a 1px hairline frame at 22%.

### Q3. Hole-hunting

Every gap below is decided here. Any gap not listed is still a hole until a voice decides it; the build does not invent a treatment at build time (QW).

**D21. Missing local file (DESIGN).** Confirmed missing: the source disappears from the switcher; YouTube becomes primary; if no source plays, the calm error treatment with the closed Details disclosure plus a link to the next lesson ("This lesson isn't available on this device" / "هذا الدرس غير متاح على هذا الجهاز"). File found but playback fails: local stays primary, YouTube secondary beside "Open in another player", the dead native video element is removed from layout (no black rectangle, no hidden audio), no auto-switch loop. Neither file nor resolvable ID: explicit unavailable state, no dead player, no raw path on screen. A failed availability probe is treated as unknown cause, never labelled missing (QW).

**D22. YouTube failure inside the page (DESIGN).** On player error, no network, or unavailable video, the player offers the hosted copy first, then the local file, in that order, with the same plain wording in both languages (QW, KM, CX). If YouTube is blocked: "YouTube is blocked right now. Try a local copy or check your connection." with Retry (KM). If the video was removed: "This YouTube lesson is no longer available." then silent check of local and R2 (KM). A late local file never auto-switches: a quiet chip, "Local copy now available. Switch." (KM). **B2 test gate:** YouTube embeds may fail from local files (no referrer) — test on Chrome, Edge, and Safari before the player is built (CC).

**D23. Hosted copy unreachable (DESIGN).** The hosted option shows only when a hosted copy exists for that video; if it is missing or fails, the option is omitted from the switcher, not shown disabled (QW). R2 failure is contained; play local if present (KM).

**D24. Offline (DESIGN).** No banner over the whole app. A notice appears only where an action needs the network (Google button, YouTube source, hosted copy) (CC). Local mode makes zero external requests; the item-9 guard is re-scoped by item 13 so the YouTube iframe is allowed only inside YouTube-playback mode and blocked everywhere else. **Gemini's "Download needed" control is REJECTED**: it rests on connection detection (`navigator.onLine` gating is forbidden) and promises a download feature that does not exist in V5. An offline lesson with no local file shows "This video is not available offline." / "هذا الفيديو غير متاح دون اتصال." with Try again (DS).

**D25. No drive connected (DESIGN).** The library opens and browses normally. Every lesson page still offers YouTube in-page and the hosted copy. The missing videos folder is detected once at startup and shown as one calm banner on the Library page using the existing `err_nofolder` string, not lesson by lesson (QW). Local actions become status text until a drive is present.

**D26. Sign-in and sync edge states (DESIGN).** Sign-in while a video plays cannot happen (the visitor is on the opening page; nothing is active). An abandoned sign-in attempt leaves the opening unchanged, guest entry still works, no partial account state is kept (QW). Account switching is not a v5 feature: signing in while another account is signed in signs the first out first and returns to the opening page (QW). Sign-out mid-video is a hard stop: stop playback immediately, destroy the YouTube iframe / pause local, one quiet notice "Signed out. Progress stays on this device." (KM). A signed-in visitor who goes offline stays signed in, saves locally, syncs when back online; furthest position wins, newest update breaks ties (CC). For an established signed-in session, offline progress queues only in that account's namespace; on sign-out or expiry, clear the visible account rail immediately and protect queued progress until the same account returns; never show another account's progress during switching (CX). Second device: no local progress loads account progress; local guest progress triggers the merge prompt; both present applies union plus furthest-position (DS).

**D27. Empty states (DESIGN).** Continue rail: absent when empty, never present-but-empty. Zero progress shows a curated first step ("Start with the first lesson" / "ابدأ بالدرس الأول"), never an empty rail (KM, DS). Saved empty keeps the v4 double-rule frame with corner stars. Search with no results says what found nothing ("No matching series" / "لا توجد سلاسل مطابقة" when series found nothing, while the lesson count above stays visually primary), plus a quiet "Browse the library" / "تصفح المكتبة" link beside "Clear search" (QW). **Gemini's generic "No lessons found" is REJECTED**: it repeats the v4 contradiction the audit flagged. Books before the first approved book: the whole section is hidden, including its links; no empty Books page, no "no books yet" copy. About with no verified bio facts: the bio block is omitted; the page shows monogram, name, role line, archive stats, and series index only. A series with zero lessons after reconciliation is omitted from rails and the grid, flagged in the build log, never shown as an empty card (QW). A book file that fails to open shows the native reader's own behaviour plus the "Open file" fallback; the app never shows a raw file path. A listed book whose file later disappears is removed from the grid; a cached detail page shows "This book is not on this device." (KM).

**D28. Guest states (DESIGN).** Guest entry works fully offline with local-only progress exactly as v4; guest progress restores on reopen from the same localStorage keys, unchanged (QW, CX). A guest never sees another account's progress, and a signed-in visitor never sees local guest progress from a different device unless merged. **No persistent sign-in banner, no "Guest Mode" banner, no "guest progress will be lost" warning anywhere** (DS, QW corrected). The guest state is shown only by the "ضيف" chip and the local Continue rail. Sign-in invitation lives in Settings/account menu and the merge prompt only.

**D29. Books and About edge states (DESIGN).** A book without a cover uses the khatam emblem, never placeholder art. A book without an English title renders its Arabic title in both EN and AR modes; no "(Untitled)" (KM). A duplicate book is flagged to the owner review list and never listed, per the dedup rule. A direct URL to hidden Books returns "Page not found" with "Return home" (DS); do not show "Books are not available yet." A scanned PDF carries its documented limits in metadata, not on screen (QW).

**D30. Transcript edge states (DESIGN).** One language missing: that tab is disabled and reads "No English transcript for this lesson" / "لا يتوفر نص بالإنجليزية لهذا الدرس" (CC). Both missing: one short status below the player. Load failure: inline error with retry ("The transcript could not load. Try again." / "تعذر تحميل النص. حاول مرة أخرى.") (DS). No placeholder transcript.

**D31. Recitation and video audio (DESIGN).** Recitation never plays on its own and stops on any video play or lesson page (CC). Enabling recitation pauses video audio; video audio resumes only by user action, with the inline sentence "Video audio paused during recitation." / "تم إيقاف صوت الفيديو أثناء التلاوة." (DS).

**D32. 390px, reduced motion, themes, mixed direction (DESIGN).** At 390px: nav stays 5 items with the clipping fixed; theme swatch panel fits the viewport; pillar-card meta never wraps a lone letter; lesson action area wraps to two rows with 44px targets; opening pills stack full width; Books grid is one column; transcript panel full width below the player; merge dialog full width with 16px margins and stacked buttons; nothing requires horizontal page scroll. Reduced motion and Calm: one CSS block collapses ambient, entrance, stagger, and draw-on durations to 0.01ms so WAAPI finished promises still resolve; ambient, parallax, and shimmer become animation none at the final frame; View Transitions and WAAPI sequences are gated behind matchMedia prefers-reduced-motion; the buffering ring becomes a static icon with a label; the Calm toggle forces this path regardless of OS setting and is always visible (QW). All six themes: every new surface checked in all six themes, both languages, at 390px and desktop; gold line contrast at least 4.5:1 against its background in every theme (QW) — the 3:1 floor from D18 is the minimum, 4.5:1 is the target for gold lines on new surfaces; Sunset is dark only and never the default; Pure dark is the accessibility theme. Mixed direction: every Arabic string with embedded Latin (Gmail, YouTube, R2, video IDs, URLs, durations, counts) isolates the Latin run; video IDs and URLs are never translated and never reordered; the EN|AR toggle keeps its labels in fixed order with only the highlight sliding, stays in the same corner on every screen, and preserves scroll position and the current video on switch (QW).

**D33. "Notify the team" is DROPPED.** Kimi's state-4 action "Notify the team" / "أبلغ الفريق" has no channel: no contact or corrections route exists in the v4 app. It cannot ship as a dead button. State 4 shows the notice plus "Browse related lessons" only. If the owner wants a feedback channel, that is a new item, not a design decision.

**D34. Counts before reconciliation (DESIGN).** Until item 1 is signed off, every visible number describes the current local catalog with its unit, and no claim of final archive coverage is shown. The About stat row shows lessons, playlists, and series with units only. "Total years" and "years of publishing" stay dropped. Hours appear only if per-lesson durations verify as a data field (QW).

## Blockers and owner decisions (not design — these go to the owner)

**B1. Google sign-in needs a sync store and a Google Cloud project. (CC, DS, KM — unanimous among those who addressed it.)** Google sign-in only accepts registered web origins (http/https); a file opened straight from disk (`file://`) is not one, and sign-in always needs a connection. Progress following the person to other devices needs a small server-side store. Building one is new infrastructure: the owner must approve it, free tier only, before item 15 is built. Recommended default: Cloudflare Worker + D1, or Supabase with Postgres RLS (DS) — per-account row ownership on every table, HttpOnly SameSite cookie, CSPRNG tokens only, no secrets in frontend, RLS-view audit. **If the owner does not approve a sync store, item 15 becomes guest-only**: cut sign-in, ship guest-only with progress saved on the device, hide nothing behind an account (CC). A sign-in that does nothing a guest can't do is a flaw. **Hard stop: no Google Sign-In engineering starts until the owner rules on B1** (CC, DS, KM).

**B2. YouTube embeds may fail from local files. (CC.)** YouTube embeds have been refusing pages that send no referrer, and local files send none. Test on Chrome, Edge, and Safari before the player is built. If it fails, the player switches by itself to the next available source (hosted copy, then local file) with one line: "تعذّر تشغيل YouTube هنا. جارٍ التشغيل من نسخة محفوظة." / "YouTube can't play here. Playing a saved copy."

**B3. Button label: Google vs Gmail. (Council 4–2 for Google; owner said Gmail.)** The council recommends "Sign in with Google"; the owner said "sign in with Gmail" twice on the call. The owner confirms the label before item 15 is built.

**B4. Phone delivery. (CC.)** A single local file isn't reliable on iOS Safari (storage, opening it at all). Owner decision on how the phone version is delivered. Same root problem as B1.

**B5. Hosted R2 access shape. (CC.)** Public read-only bucket, or links signed on the server. No keys in the app. Owner/build to confirm which one.
