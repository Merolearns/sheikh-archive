## (5) Risks and checks before building

- A naïve replacement of V4’s external-URL guard can let network dependencies slip in. Scope the exception to validated YouTube lesson anchors. Forbid remote embeds, assets, scripts, preconnect, prefetch and fetch. Capture outgoing traffic with networking available and again with networking disabled. Before activation, require zero requests outside the local archive. For activation, verify only the explicit new-tab navigation leaves the archive context. Inject a remote asset and a malformed link to prove the guards fail.
- Route changes and delayed media errors can promote the wrong action or hide a playable file. Drive action state from the current lesson and ignore stale probes. Test 404, confirmed file with unsupported format, unavailable probe, same-lesson rerender, rapid lesson changes and keyboard activation.
- Build string checks should cover both locales, empty/error states, and the reader, with injected em dash, draft text and AI credit lines proving the checks can fail. Retain the byte/hash check on the bundled CC0 recitation and the zero-request check on both installed entry paths. Preserve watch and resume keys through catalog corrections.
- Freeze the inventory snapshot for reconciliation. A net delta of 12 can hide offsetting differences, and matching membership totals alone can hide duplicated IDs or order changes. Check navigation through repeated memberships and path resolution for Arabic names and percent-encoded brackets.

## (6) Explicit exclusions

- Other-scholars section or a curated other-speaker rail. The existing ordinary access to the Bayoun playlist serves the catalog without shifting the library’s focus.
- Remote player, thumbnails, remote readers, analytics, accounts, or auto-opening YouTube on local error. These add runtime dependencies or bypass the visitor’s choice.
- Invented titles, filenames, authors, descriptions or credentials. Use supplied book records and claim-level evidence.
- New recordings, AI audio or credits, paid keys, purchases, third-party clouds, or reopening playbook §8 decisions. The shipped recitation and local delivery already meet the project constraints. Keep the font and CC0 provenance credits.
- Catalog-wide retagging or a motion redesign. Spend the review budget on the seven owner decisions, count integrity, and regressions in the shipped bilingual experience.
