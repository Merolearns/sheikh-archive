# English-title glossary and style rules — Sheikh archive (v5)

Authoritative for series + video `titleEn`. One Arabic term = one English
rendering, everywhere. Decided Nov 22, 2026 (translation fix). Qur'an wording
follows Sahih International.

## Style rules

- Translate, do not transliterate, except for proper names and terms with no
  safe English equivalent (see list). Keep proper names transliterated
  consistently: Sheikh Moustafa Kamel, Qur'an, Seerah, Sunna, Fiqh, Tajweed,
  Tawhid, Aqeedah, Shama'il, Hajj, Zakat, Taraweeh, Isha, Fajr.
- شرح = "Explanation of" (a commentary on a book). مختصر = "Abridgement of".
  تفسير = "Tafsir of". باب = "Chapter". كتاب (inside Sahih al-Bukhari) = "Book".
  الحديث رقم = "Hadith no.". الدرس = "Lesson", دروس = "Lessons".
  خطبة / خطب = "Sermon" / "Sermons". الحلقة = "Episode". المحاضرة = "Lecture".
  الجزء = "Part" for book parts, "Juz'" for Qur'an parts.
- Surah names use standard English names: Surah al-Fatiha, al-Baqarah,
  Maryam, al-An'am, al-Kahf, etc. سورة = "Surah".
- Honorifics: ﷺ is kept as the glyph ﷺ. صلى الله عليه وسلم = "peace be upon
  him". عليه السلام = "peace be upon him". رضي الله عنه = "may Allah be
  pleased with him", عنها = "may Allah be pleased with her",
  عنهم = "may Allah be pleased with them".
- Arabic-Indic digits (٠-٩) become Latin digits (0-9) in English titles.
  Hijri years keep the AH marker where the Arabic has هـ; Gregorian years
  keep CE where the Arabic has م.
- "سؤال وجواب" = "Questions and Answers". "سلسلة" = "Series" (kept only where
  it reads naturally; "The ... Series"). "خواطر" = "Reflections".
  "قصص وعبر" = "Stories and Lessons". "وقفات تربوية" = "Educational Reflections".
- Ruling words keep their modal force: حكم = "ruling", يجوز = "permissible",
  لا يجوز = "not permissible", حرام = "forbidden", حلال = "lawful".
  Never soften a ruling ("must" vs "may" must follow the Arabic).
- Qur'an verse fragments inside titles use Sahih International wording,
  quoted, with an ellipsis where the Arabic truncates ("..").
- Numbers stay attached to what they number (lesson no., hadith no., page
  no.). Do not reorder numbering.
- A title that is already Latin script (dates, guest lectures in English)
  is copied unchanged into `titleEn`.
- If the Arabic is garbled, truncated past understanding, or a word cannot
  be identified with confidence, the title is HELD (no `titleEn`) — never guess.
